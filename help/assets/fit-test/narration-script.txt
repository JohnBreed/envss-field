00:00:00  Open the app
This is a quick guide to recording a respirator fit test in the ENVSS Field app on a Chromebook. You'll need the job number, and a photo of the PortaCount fit test report. The job and names in this video are example data.

00:00:16  Open the job
Sign in with your ENVSS Google account as usual, then click the job on the Projects screen. We're using job 0 0 1 9 3 8 9.

00:00:27  New fit-testing event
Click New project event. Under Event type, choose Respirator fit testing, check the date, and click Create event.

00:00:42  Event number
The app numbers it for you: the job number, then FIT, then 0 0 1. The next fit-testing event on this job will be FIT 0 0 2.

00:00:53  Add the report photo
Now click Add individual report, and choose the photo of the TSI report. On a phone this opens the camera. On the Chromebook, pick the photo from Files. Get the whole report in the shot, flat and in focus.

00:01:09  Wait for the read
The app reads the photo with Google Vision. Give it a few seconds, and stay on this screen.

00:01:16  Confirm screen
Next is the confirm screen. The photo is on the left, and the fields from the report are filled in on the right: the ID number, name, company, test and due dates, the PortaCount serial number, and the respirator.

00:01:32  Check every field
Scroll down to check the protocol, pass level, the four exercises, the overall fit factor, and the PASS or FAIL result. Anything the app isn't sure about is highlighted. Check every field against the photo, and fix anything that's wrong. The person who was tested still needs to sign; your ENVSS sign-off is filled in from your sign-in.

00:01:51  Signing later
If you can't get the person's signature straight away, click Save without signing. You'll get an unsigned PDF, and you can open the report later to sign it.

00:02:02  Continue to signature
Otherwise, when everything's correct, click Continue to signature.

00:02:07  Technician sign-off  [VIDEO AT 2:07–2:24 STILL SHOWS THE OLD HAND-SIGNED TECH PAD]
Your ENVSS sign-off is filled in automatically from your Google sign-in: your name, your envss.com.au email, and the date and time in AWST. You do not need to sign.

00:02:15  Person tested signs
Hand the device to the person who was tested, and they sign in the Person tested box. Use Clear if you need to start again.

00:02:25  Save signed PDF
Click Save signed PDF.

00:02:28  The signed PDF
The app makes a one page PDF in the TSI report layout, with the ENVSS header, your electronic sign-off, and the individual's signature, and downloads a copy. It's named by surname, first name, mask model and test date.

00:02:43  Copied to Drive
Back on the event, the report shows as signed. Under Files for Drive, the PDF is copied as a new file into the Fit-Tests folder in the job's Field App Drive folder. Nothing already in the job folder is changed.

00:02:59  Finish the event
Add a report for each person you test. When everyone has signed, click Mark event complete. If you need this guide again, there's a How to link at the bottom of every fit-testing event.
